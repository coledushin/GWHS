Question quality difficulty vs. point-biserial

How to read point-biserial (PB)

PB shows whether a question separates strong students from struggling ones: the correlation between getting it right and scoring well on the rest of the test. Higher is better, and a negative value usually means a wrong answer key or a misleading distractor.

  • Strong0.30+separates students well
  • Acceptable0.20–0.29fine as is
  • Weak0.10–0.19check wording and distractors
  • Poorbelow 0.10check the answer key
